THE DIRECTORATE

COLONEL JB AJANAKU (RTD) - DIRECTOR

Joseph Bamidele Ajanaku was born on 18th March 1962 at Ibadan, South West Nigeria. He hails from Oka in Akoko South West Local Government Area of Ondo State. He attended Nigerian Military School, Zaria (1975-80) and the University of Jos, Jos (1980-84). He was a recipient of the Nigerian Government Merit Award for academic excellence in 1980. He graduated in 1984 with a BSc (Hons) from the Department of Geography and Planning and was commissioned into the Nigerian Army Education Corps as a Second Lieutenant on 4th October, 1989. He rose through the officer cadre and was promoted to the rank of Colonel in September 2012.

With about 26 years of teaching experience, Col JB Ajanaku (rtd) taught Mathematics, Geography and Economics at the Nigerian Military School, Zaria and Command Day Secondary School, Rukuba Jos respectively. He also served as Commandant of different Command Schools as well as Chief Inspector of schools. As the Divisional Education Officer at Army Headquarters, Garrison, he trained over 30 Quality Assurance Evaluators for the Nigerian Army and the Federal Ministry of Defence. He was also involved as a facilitator and resource person in several training workshops for teachers across Nigeria.

Col JB Ajanaku (rtd) is a recipient of the Forces Service Star (FSS), Meritorious Service Star (MSS) and Training Support Medal (TSM) medals of the Nigerian Army in recognition of his innovative and ground-breaking contributions to the development of training at various levels in and out of the army especially in the Command School System. He also belongs to many professional associations including Nigerian Society of International Affairs, Nigerian Geographical Society, Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria and Nigerian Institute of Management.

A versatile, engaging and seminal motivational public speaking professional, Col JB Ajanaku (rtd) is a leadership teacher and coach, specializing in the study of leadership failure and prevention. He is also a trainer and coach in Teacher Education, Individual and Corporate Training and Development as well as Performance and Productivity Enhancement in the Workplace. He is a trainer and coach in the areas of Critical Thinking, Emotional Intelligence, Team Building, Conflict Transformation and Peer Mediation. Colonel JB Ajanaku is a poet, writer, book editor, indexer and reviewer as well as a researcher who has contributed over 90 articles and paper presentations to local and international journals and at international workshops and conferences.

While in service, Col JB Ajanaku (rtd) co-authored a book for the Nigerian Army Education Corps titled: Principle, Process and Practice of School Inspection. He has also co-authored a TETFUND-sponsored book titled The Total Teacher: 70 questions teachers need to answer to remain effective in the classroom. He has vast experience as a school administrator, classroom teacher, adolescent mentor, motivator, training facilitator and Education Quality Assurance Evaluator. Col JB Ajanaku retired voluntarily from the Nigerian Army on 31st March 2017. He is now actively involved in training and consultancy as the CEO of Great Thinkers Global Resources Limited, a firm specializing in the provision of performance and productivity improvement training services for individuals, schools, training institutions and corporate bodies. His vision is “to transform the workplace environment through purposeful leadership and creative empowerment programmes for effective performance and enhanced productivity.”

ENGR HARUNA ACHEKA BITRUS JP - DIRECTOR

He is a holder of a Bachelor of Engineering in Agriculture, COREN registered Engineer, a Fellow of the Institute of Credit Administration. and member of the Nigerian Society of Engineers, member of the Nigerian Institute of Management. He was a lecturer at College of Agric Zuru before joining the NACB which is currently the Bank of Agriculture in 1987 where he rose from Officer to the rank of a Deputy General Manager. Before his retirement from the Bank in the year 2020, he attended several courses on Personnel and Financial Management, Project Formulation, Supervision/Monitoring and Audit. He held several management positions and served as a Zonal Head of Internal Audit in the bank.

MARYAM LADI LABBO – DIRECTOR/COMPANY SECRETARY

Maryam is a highly result oriented trained professional with technical and sound analytical skills who enjoys challenges and accurately prioritizes a complex workload to meet deadlines. She is extensively experienced in leading high-impact projects and delivering measurable value to clients. She is a credit analyst and agribusiness development expert. She holds a Bachelors degree in agriculture and a Masters in Business Administration from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria.

She joined the services of Bank of Agriculture in 1991 and retired in 2025 as an Assistant General Manager after an eventful and successful career spanning close to 35 years. She worked in various times at the bank‟s branch (field) offices, projects appraisal and implementation, credit administration, risk management, audit and partnerships/collaboration departments.

While in service, she carried out the following special assignments:

  • Permanent member of the bank‟s recurrent budget committee
  • Member of several committees on loan monitoring and recovery
  • Pioneer desk officer of the bank‟s Youth and Gender Desk.
  • Member, Committee on Implementation of the Presidency Agricultural Transformation Agenda Private Sector driven Mechanization Intervention program
  • Member, Committee on Implementation of the „ Nigeria Sugar Master Plan‟ in collaboration with the Nigeria Sugar Development Council to boost sugar production in the country.

Maryam is an recipient of the Certificate on Strategic Leadership, Ethics and Policy Making from the Nigeria‟s National Institute for Policy and Strategic Studies (NIPSS). In December 2025, she was decorated with the prestigious “Integrity Ambassador” of the National Institute for Policy and Strategic Studies (NIPSS).

She holds membership of the following professional bodies:

  • Member, National Institute for Credit Administration (NICA)
  • Member, Agricultural Society of Nigeria (ASN)
  • Senior Associate, Chartered Risk Management Institute (CRMI)
